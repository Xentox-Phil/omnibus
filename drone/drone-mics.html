Drone Acoustic Array — 4-Mic Tetrahedral

Minimum mics for unambiguous 3D direction-of-arrival: 4, non-coplanar.

Placement

Front-Left & Front-Right: below arm (−Z)

Back-Left & Back-Right: above arm (+Z)

Four mics form a tetrahedron — no two on the same plane → resolves azimuth + elevation without front/back or up/down mirror ambiguity.

Why this geometry

• Baseline ≈ 2 × arm (~40–60 cm)
• Good for 0.5–8 kHz drone harmonics
• Non-coplanar removes the cone-of-confusion
• Boom-mount below/above prop plane → ~6–10 dB lower own-noise vs in-plane

SNR tip

Mount on rigid booms ~5–10 cm offset from arm, foam windscreens, reference mic at each prop hub for ESC-telemetry-driven noise cancellation.

Non-coplanar is the whole point. If all 4 mics sit at Z = 0 you cannot distinguish "above" from "below" — same time-delays. Offset 2 mics up, 2 down by ≥10 cm.

Why these numbers

  • 40 cm baseline in X/Y → time-delay max ≈ 40 cm / 343 m/s ≈ 1.17 ms. At 16 kHz sampling that's ~19 samples — plenty of resolution for GCC-PHAT.
  • 20 cm vertical separation → enough to break the up/down ambiguity without making the array fragile.
  • Mic foam windscreens (the cheap fluffy ones from a Zoom recorder, ~$5) are non-negotiable during motors-off freefall.
  • Rubber-isolated mounts (small silicone grommets) — airframe ringdown after motor cutoff is real, ~50–100 ms.

Parts (cheap path)

  • 4× MEMS I2S mics (e.g. INMP441) — ~$3 each
  • Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W or Teensy 4.1 with 4-channel I2S
  • OR shortcut: ReSpeaker 4-Mic Array for Pi — $25, plug-and-play, but fixed geometry (~6 cm baseline → worse DOA resolution but works at 10 m)
  • Carbon-fiber tube booms (10 cm), zip ties, foam windscreens

DSP pipeline

  1. Capture 4-ch @ 16 kHz, 250 ms window
  2. Band-pass 200 Hz – 4 kHz (drone harmonics)
  3. GCC-PHAT on all 6 mic pairs → 6 time delays
  4. Least-squares fit for unit direction vector (azimuth + elevation)
  5. Output bearing, repeat next hop

2-Day demo: honest take

Full airborne motors-off hopper in 2 days: no. Flight controller integration alone is >2 days.
Convincing static demo of the DOA core in 2 days: yes.

Realistic 2-day scope:

  • Day 1 AM: ReSpeaker 4-mic on a Pi (or laptop + USB audio interface). Mount on a tripod / broomstick.
  • Day 1 PM: GCC-PHAT in Python (numpy + scipy). Test with handclaps → bearing on screen.
  • Day 2 AM: Hover a target drone 5–10 m away outside. Record + live-classify bearing. Plot bearing trace over time.
  • Day 2 PM: Polish viz, record a 30-sec demo video. Optional: mount on a stationary drone, motors off, repeat to prove airframe doesn't break it.

Skip for the demo: in-flight motor cutoff, flight controller integration, custom PCB, ESC-telemetry noise cancel. Those are weeks, not days.

Biggest risk in 2 days: wind. If it's gusty on demo day your bearing trace will be garbage. Have a fallback indoor venue or shield the mics.

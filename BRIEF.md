# Omnibus — Hackathon Brief (das Stadtwerk.Mobilität / RVV)

Transcribed from the challenge slides.

## NEXT STOP — Harnessing Big Data from Regensburg's Buses

*das Stadtwerk.Mobilität* keeps Regensburg moving — **> 6 million kilometers in 2025**.

Every single bus acts as a **rolling sensor**, constantly streaming live data.

We have prepared that data for you. Dive into our Big Data, analyzing millions of
data points, and help us **build real-world solutions** to make Regensburg's public
transit more **reliable, smarter, and greener**.

## OUR WISHES

We don't expect a finished software product. What we want to see is a **functional
prototype** — whether it's an interactive dashboard, a striking visualization, or a
clever algorithm.

- **The Regensburg Factor:** Does your design solve a specific, real-world mobility
  problem in our city?
- **Innovation:** How creatively do you handle the ITCS data? *"Surprise us!"*
- **Technical Execution:** How efficiently do you process these massive Big Data
  streams? Combining our resources with external data sets? We highly encourage it.
